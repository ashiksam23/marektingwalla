Day 3: Active Recovery / Rest

Today is for light activity or full rest. This is crucial for muscle repair and preventing burnout.

  • Engage in a light walk (20-30 minutes).
  • Perform dynamic stretching or a full-body yoga flow.
  • Use a foam roller for any sore areas.
  • Or, take a complete rest day. Listen to your body.

Day 5: Full Body Conditioning (HIRT)

This is a circuit-style day focusing on compound movements and cardio integration. Perform 3-4 rounds of the following circuit, resting 60 seconds between rounds.

  • Kettlebell Swings 15 reps (Explosive Hips/Posterior Chain)
  • Goblet Squats 12 reps (Focus on depth and posture)
  • Burpees 10 reps (Full Body Cardio)
  • Mountain Climbers 45 seconds
  • Russian Twists 20 reps (10 per side)

Day 6: Cardio & Core

Finish the week strong with sustained cardio and a dedicated core circuit. This builds endurance and strengthens your midline.

Cardio

30-45 minutes of sustained effort (e.g., a brisk run, cycling, elliptical, or jumping rope) at a heart rate of 60-75% of your maximum.

Core Circuit (Repeat 3 times)

  • Leg Raises: 15 reps
  • Side Planks: 30 seconds per side
  • Bicycle Crunches: 20 reps (10 per side)
